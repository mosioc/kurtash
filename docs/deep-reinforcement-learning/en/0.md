# Grokking Deep Reinforcement Learning — Book Outline

**Grokking Deep Reinforcement Learning** by Miguel Morales teaches the concepts of deep reinforcement learning, from mathematical foundations and classical algorithms to advanced Deep RL methods.

To study the book effectively, each chapter is best approached in three parts:

1. **Main topics:** What is the chapter about?
2. **Key concepts:** Algorithms, terminology, and core ideas.
3. **Application and relevance:** What problem does each concept solve, and where does it fit within Deep RL?

## **Complete Table of Contents and Chapter Descriptions**

The book has 13 chapters, which can be divided into four parts.

## **Part 1: Foundations of Reinforcement Learning**

### **Chapter 1. Introduction to Deep Reinforcement Learning**

Fundamentals and overview

An introduction to the concept of deep reinforcement learning, how it differs from other branches of machine learning, and its applications.

Key topics:

- Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
- Differences between Supervised, Unsupervised, and Reinforcement Learning
- The Agent and Environment concepts
- Trial-and-Error Learning
- Sequential Feedback and Evaluative Feedback
- Function Approximation
- History and future of Deep RL
- Advantages and limitations of Deep Reinforcement Learning

**Chapter goal:** Understand what Deep RL is, what problems it solves, and why neural networks are used in it.

### **Chapter 2. Mathematical Foundations of Reinforcement Learning**

(A very important chapter)

This chapter introduces the mathematical foundations and the structure of reinforcement learning environments.

Key topics:

- Agent and Environment
- State and Action
- Reward and Reward Signal
- Transition Function
- Agent–Environment Interaction
- Markov Decision Process (MDP)
- State Space and Action Space
- Horizon
- Discount Factor (\(\gamma\))
- Stochastic Environments

**Chapter goal:** Understand the structure of an RL problem and how an agent interacts with its environment.

### **Chapter 3. Balancing Immediate and Long-Term Goals**

(A very important chapter)

This chapter addresses how an agent balances immediate rewards against long-term rewards.

Key topics:

- Policy (\(\pi\))
- State-Value Function (\(V(s)\))
- Action-Value Function (\(Q(s,a)\))
- Advantage Function (\(A(s,a)\))
- Optimal Policy
- Policy Evaluation
- Policy Improvement
- Policy Iteration
- Value Iteration

**Chapter goal:** Learn how to evaluate decisions and find the optimal policy.

### **Chapter 4. Balancing the Gathering and Use of Information**

(A very important chapter)

One of the central challenges in RL is deciding when an agent should gather new information and when it should exploit its current knowledge.

Key topics:

- Multi-Armed Bandit
- Exploration and Exploitation
- Regret
- Greedy Strategy
- Random Exploration
- Epsilon-Greedy

- Optimistic Initialization

- Upper Confidence Bound (UCB)
- Thompson Sampling

**Chapter goal:** Understand the exploration–exploitation dilemma and the various methods for solving it.

### **Chapter 5. Evaluating Agents' Behaviors**

(A very important chapter)

This chapter introduces methods for estimating the value of a policy. In other words, how does an agent determine how good its decisions are?

Key topics:

- Policy Evaluation
- Monte Carlo Methods
- First-Visit Monte Carlo
- Every-Visit Monte Carlo
- Temporal Difference Learning (TD)
- Bootstrapping
- N-Step TD Learning

- TD(\(\lambda\))
- Eligibility Traces

### **Chapter 6. Improving Agents' Behaviors**

### **Chapter 7. Achieving Goals More Effectively and Efficiently**
